prepare review PR, build in GitHub Actions; no merge or release.

Media3 service, PlayerConnection, queues, downloads, synchronization,
recognition and social routes. Only Android applicationId becomes
com.onyx.music. Keep GPL-3.0 and upstream credits.

Visual design: black background, #0B0B0B surfaces, #111111 cards,
#222222 borders, #F5F5F5 text, #999999 secondary, white accent.

Use platform sans typography, rounded 12–24dp shapes, 48dp hit targets,
restrained animation and existing responsive player-aware insets.
